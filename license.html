DIGITAL PRODUCT LICENSE

Know the terms before you use it.

Draft for owner and legal review. The creator’s license for each listing must be specified before sale. Do not assume a product is licensed for commercial use just because it can be downloaded.

Listing-specific permission

Review the license and restrictions attached to the individual product before purchase. The listing should identify whether personal, editorial, or commercial use is permitted, along with any limits on redistribution, resale, attribution, modification, or use in templates and stock collections.

Ownership and permitted use

Downloading a file does not transfer copyright or ownership. A license only grants the rights expressly stated by the creator for that product. If a listing does not provide clear license terms, ask [support email / creator contact] before using the file beyond evaluating the listing.
