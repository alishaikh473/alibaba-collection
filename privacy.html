Information involved in using the site

The site stores saved items, cart contents, and display preference in your browser’s local storage. If you sign in as an administrator, Supabase Auth processes account credentials and the current application keeps session tokens in memory for the tab.

When a purchase is configured, Stripe processes checkout and payment details on its hosted pages. DiscoveryImage’s Supabase backend records order information needed to verify payment and provide the purchased download, including checkout identifiers, order items, amount, currency, and customer email when supplied by Stripe.

Service providers and external requests

Supabase provides application database, authentication, and file storage services. Stripe provides payment processing. The site may request fonts from Google Fonts and sample/creator images from their configured image hosts. Those providers may receive technical request information under their own privacy terms.

Use, retention, and choices

Order information is used to record verified purchases, support delivery, and address order questions. Order and transaction records may be retained for accounting, dispute, and legal requirements. Do not remove order records without considering those obligations. Browser-saved items can be cleared through your browser’s storage controls.
